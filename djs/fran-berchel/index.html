Bio Trayectoria

Franberchel es DJ profesional desde 2014 y ha tocado en escenarios importantes de todo México.

Uno de los momentos clave de su carrera fue convertirse en DJ residente de OMNIA Los Cabos, el beach club del grupo Hakkasan, donde compartió cabina con artistas internacionales como Martin Garrix, Kaskade, Oliver Heldens y Claptone. Ahí grabó 54 podcasts de sus sets en Feel Good Fridays at OMNIA.

También ha participado en causas como Heroes Fest, un festival para recaudar fondos a beneficio de niños con necesidades especiales.

Read in English

Franberchel has been a professional DJ since 2014, playing major stages across Mexico.

A career highlight was becoming a resident DJ at OMNIA Los Cabos, the Hakkasan Group beach club, where he shared the stage with international artists such as Martin Garrix, Kaskade, Oliver Heldens and Claptone, and recorded 54 podcasts of his Feel Good Fridays at OMNIA sets.

He has also supported charitable causes such as Heroes Fest, a festival raising funds for children with special needs.
